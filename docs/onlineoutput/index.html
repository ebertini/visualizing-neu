Northeastern University Grant Visualization

Analysis and visualization of faculty grants, collaborations, and research funding at Northeastern University.

Visualizations and Insights

  • Grant AtlasInteractive
    Interactive map of all NEU grants — explore by agency, college, faculty, and funding amount.
  • Topic HierarchyInteractive
    Hierarchical view of research topics derived from grant abstracts; drill into sub-topics and see funding distribution.
  • Topic IslandsInteractive
    UMAP-based topic landscape showing clusters of related grants as navigable islands.

Notebooks

  • 01 · Schema Overview
    Load every processed table, print schemas / nulls / samples; sanity-check the faculty ↔ grants ↔ abstracts joins.
  • 02 · Funding Landscape
    Baseline distributions: grant size (linear + log), duration, yearly count, agency frequency, faculty rank & tenure, co-PI rate.
  • 03 · Funding Over Time
    Annual & cumulative funding 2000–2025, rolling averages, college × year heatmap, agency mix over time, pre/post-COVID comparison.
  • 04 · Who Gets Funded
    Concentration analysis: Lorenz curve / Gini, top-25 faculty, top-15 departments, agency × department matrix, plus the earned-at-NEU vs prior-institution attribution comparison.
  • 05 · Collaboration Network
    Co-PI network: NetworkX graph, degree / betweenness / PageRank centrality, Louvain communities, cross-college collaboration matrix, trend over time.
  • 06 · Research Topics
    LDA topic model (k=8) over grant abstracts: perplexity sweep, UMass coherence & confidence validation, topic prevalence + trends over time, topics × college / agency / funding, top faculty per topic.
  • 07 · Topic Deep Dive
    Follow-up analyses: sub-topics inside each parent (LDA k=4), JS-distance topic dendrogram, per-college profile cards, and UMAP projections (TF-IDF baseline + SPECTER2 — produces the two interactive projections above).

Data analyzed from faculty lists and grant records (2025–2026)
